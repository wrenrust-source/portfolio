About

I am an aspiring environmental engineer with a passion for combining engineering, design, and sustainability. My background in visual communication and design complements my technical skills in CAD, ArcGIS, and engineering design, allowing me to develop solutions and communicate ideas that are both functional and visually compelling.

My research experience includes NOAA's East Coast Ocean Acidification cruises, where I specialized in measuring total alkalinity. I enjoy transforming ideas into innovative, sustainable designs that create meaningful environmental impact.

Research Experience

NOAA's 2026 East Coast Ocean Acidification Cruise

As a member of the NOAA East Coast Ocean Acidification research team, I participated in field and laboratory operations supporting the monitoring of changing ocean chemistry. Working aboard NOAA’s Henry B. Bigelow fishery research vessel, I specialized in the measurement of total alkalinity, helping to collect and analyze seawater samples to generate high-quality data used to study ocean acidification and its impacts on marine ecosystems.

Skills: Seawater Chemistry · Environmental Research · Titration · pH Calibration · Data Analysis

Independent Research: Native Plant Names and Marketability

Investigated how plant terminology influences consumer perception and willingness to purchase native plants. Analyzed 273 survey responses using statistical tests to compare reactions to “weed” versus positive naming conventions, evaluating implications for native plant adoption and environmental communication.

Survey Research · Statistical Analysis · Two-Proportion Z-Tests · Data Analysis · Environmental Communication
