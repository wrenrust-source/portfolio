Engineers Without Borders: Water Filtration for Communities in Malawi

The University of Delaware's Engineers Without Borders research team is developing low-cost ceramic water filtration systems for communities in Malawi to address E. coli and chemical contaminants. As part of the research team, I am investigating how ceramic porosity, firing temperature, and biochar integration affect filter design and performance.

The goal is to develop a ceramic filter model that integrates biochar, differentiating it from existing ceramic filters and improving its capacity to remove chemical contaminants. We are applying engineering design and materials science principles to develop an accessible water-treatment solution that communities in Malawi can produce locally and affordably.

Skills: Ceramic Porosity · Firing Temperature Optimization · Biochar Integration · Water Filtration

Independent Research: Native Plant Names and Marketability

Investigated how plant terminology influences consumer perception and willingness to purchase native plants. Analyzed 273 survey responses using statistical tests to compare reactions to “weed” versus positive naming conventions, evaluating implications for native plant adoption and environmental communication.

Skills: Survey Research · Statistical Analysis · Two-Proportion Z-Tests · Data Analysis · Environmental Communication
